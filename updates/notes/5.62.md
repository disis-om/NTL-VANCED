# Themes
Pick a wallpaper and the whole mod dresses to match.

## Themes button
- Top-right of the home screen, where slither's graphics-quality icon used to be — that icon now lives inside the panel as **GRAPHICS QUALITY** (High → Normal → Skinless, same as before)
- Nine wallpapers: Kaboom, Bikini Bottom, Spidey, Smoke, Wine, Paper Orange, Paper Crimson, Clouds, Waves — plus the Vanced default
- **Custom**: any image from your device; its palette is read from the picture

## What changes with a theme
- Every menu, popup, chat box, HUD panel, button, heading and icon takes the wallpaper's colours — accents, highlights and the dark card tints
- The wallpaper is saved with NTL's own menu-image setting, so it is there from the first frame after a reload

## Wallpaper blur
- Slider in the same panel, 0–40 px, live; **20 px by default**
- Switches itself off during a round (the arena covers it anyway)
