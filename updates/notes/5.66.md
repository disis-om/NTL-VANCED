## Vanced Skins
- New **Vanced Skins** button in the skin editor, next to Select Cosmetic
- 11 creature skins for your snake: **Centipede, Dragon, Skeleton, Chinese Dragon, Electric Eel, Train, Robot Snake, Phoenix, Ice Serpent, Caterpillar and Zombie Snake**
- Every creature has its own boost effect: fire breath, lightning, glowing eyes, thrusters, frost breath and more
- Players without NTL VANCED see the normal skin closest to your creature's colours; pick **Normal skin** to get your previous skin back
- Only the creature in use animates on the page, so it stays smooth on phones

## See each other's creatures
- NTL VANCED players in the same arena now see each other's creature skins
- Works on slither.io and slither.com, over IPv4 and IPv6
- **Share my skin** and **See others' skins** switches on the Vanced Skins page, both on by default
- Game packets are untouched; only the skin you picked is shared, and it is cleared when you die or leave
