# Center eyes, seen by everyone
Center eyes used to be a trick only you could see. Now it goes out to the arena.

## Center eyes
- Press **I** and the pupils settle in the middle on every screen — yours included
- Same engine as Eyes Back, pointed sideways: the snake still steers exactly where your mouse or arrow points, with the same tiny wobble
- **Vanced › Controls › Eyes** — switch "Center eyes visible to others" off to go back to the old client-only look (nothing is sent, pupils pinned dead centre for you alone)
- Eyes Back (**U**) and Center eyes (**I**) take over from each other; one is active at a time
