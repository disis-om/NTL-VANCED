# Assist line for arrow mode
NTL's assist line used to point at the mouse — useless on a phone. It now follows the arrow.

## Arrow control
- Turn on NTL assist while steering with the arrow: a dashed line is drawn from your snake's head to the arrow, in the arrow's colour
- It appears only while your finger is down and disappears together with the arrow
